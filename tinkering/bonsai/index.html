Growing…

A bonsai you can raise from a nursery starter. The tree grows by a botanical model: buds compete for light, growth flows to the strongest shoots, and branches thicken by the pipe model, so cuts, pinching and wire change how it grows next.

Pause at any time to work on it, then let it grow. Scrub back to an earlier year and try something different; the old future is kept as its own timeline.
