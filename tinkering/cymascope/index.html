A cymascope plays sound into a shallow dish of liquid and photographs it from above under a ring of lights. The surface can only settle into the dish’s own standing waves, so each tone draws a pattern, and the lights pick out the slopes.

This one works the waves out from the physics: the tone sets the wavelength through surface tension and gravity, and the dish’s modes near that wavelength answer, sharply or softly depending on the liquid. Drag the image to turn it, scroll to zoom.
