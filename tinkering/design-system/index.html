Colour

Draft

Three layers. The palette holds raw colours (--c-*). Roles say what a colour is for (--ground, --fg, --muted) and change with the theme. Components use roles, never raw palette values, so a theme change reaches them automatically.

The palette is a working set from Figma, with more colours than the site will keep.

Split-flap board

Settled

Design System

  • The home page’s name. One canvas per letter, each flipping round a drum of characters, drawn with motion blur so fast flaps smear like film. Letters are Geist; the plate and windows take their colours from the theme.
  • Everything about it, from timing and blur to the plate and grain, is tuned in the Split-Flap Lab, which keeps a dark and a light version. split-flap.js runs the active ones.
  • On phones the board drops its side columns, runs edge to edge and enlarges the letters. With reduced motion it shows the last frame straight away.

Call to action

The next section

Every section sets its own roles, so text, lines and tags follow its colour.

  • Layout: tagline, tags and description in a narrow left column (minmax(340px, 5fr)), the image in the rest (9fr), stacked below 800px. The call to action tucks under the image’s right corner.
  • Height: on the home page each section is at least a screen tall plus room for the transitions at both ends, so a full screen of solid colour sits between them.
  • Halftone: drawn by halftone.js on a canvas, with WebGL when it’s available, from the active version in the Halftone Lab. It may drift and respond to the cursor. It holds still for reduced motion.

Reel player

Draft
  • A product reel in a 16:9 frame. On the home page it breaks out of the column to fill the window, capped so the whole frame fits below the nav.
  • Before it plays, a glass pill sits bottom left, clear of the title card, over a soft shade. On hover the pill fills with the text colour. Once playing, the overlay fades in 250ms; when the reel ends the label turns to “Replay”.

Static spark

Draft
  • When the pointer comes close to a button, a small bolt jumps from the button’s edge to the pointer, and the button flinches brighter for 280ms. It never fires while the pointer is on the button, and only on devices with a fine pointer and without reduced motion.
  • Reach, life, jaggedness, glow and colours are set in the Button Lab with the nav buttons. It is … in the active version.

Open issues

Known problems, to fix as each piece gets settled.

  • Some nav button colours aren’t in the palette

    Mist and Rust now link to their tokens. The dark buttons’ tile colours (#10202d, #0a161f, #2c3b46), the light buttons’ top (#c35f46) and text (#eee1ce), and the shadow colours are still their own. Either add them to the palette, or swap them for the nearest palette colours in the Button Lab.

  • The type scale has near neighbours

    18, 17, 16 and 15px all appear. Intro (18) and body (17) could merge, and 16 is only used by the reel pill and the phone lead.

  • The nav is too wide between 600 and 800px

    It stays on one row and pushes the page sideways (769px wide at 700px). Wrapping it onto two rows, as on phones, would fix it.
