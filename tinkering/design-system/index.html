Design System

Matte: the paint, parts and rules this site is built from.

A system drawn from 80s and 90s matte black electronics: powder-coated bodies, machined seams, printed legends and one warm accent. Every specimen here is drawn by the stylesheets the site ships, so this page always matches it. The bar at the foot of the window tunes the tokens and every part follows; the sun and moon button shows both finishes.

01

Colour

Draft

Matte is one paint, lit from above. Every grey on a part is worked out from a single tone, --m-tone, so the body, the raised parts and the knurling stay in step when it changes. One warm accent, copper, marks what is live. The page around the parts uses the site’s roles (--ground, --fg, --muted), which say what a colour is for and change with the theme. Parts use roles and Matte tokens, never raw values.

Paint · Matte tokens as the page computes them now

Signal and status

The accent fills a part; the signal is the same colour used as a mark on a body, darkened in the light finish so it still reads. Status colours are for lamps and message text, and each is at least 4.5:1 on its body in both finishes.

Type

Draft

Söhne from Klim, in three cuts: Söhne Breit semibold for display, Söhne book for text, Söhne Mono for legends, tags, captions and metadata. Geist appears only as the split-flap board’s letters, drawn on canvas. Every size is a --text-* token. Text styles are shown here in the text colour; each page can set them in --muted instead.

Legends

On a Matte part, type is printed on the body, small and in capitals, or it glows from behind glass. Nothing else carries text.

03

Surface

Settled

A part sits at one of four heights: a well cut into the body, the body itself, a key standing on it and a knob standing proud of that. Highlights sit on upper edges and shadows fall down. The paint has a fine speckle, --m-speckle, and in a plain key that speckle is iron: it stands up to the pointer and is thrown back by a press (see the Key under Controls).

05

Motion

Draft

Short and quiet. Seven durations and five curves, kept in the site’s stylesheet and used by every part here. Duration steps up with distance. Parts go down fast and come up slower; things leave one step faster than they arrive; only parts with a physical stop overshoot. With reduced motion on, travel becomes an instant change and light still fades. Smooth scrolling only runs when the visitor hasn’t asked for reduced motion.

On the site · timings that sit outside the scale, tuned with their parts

  • Card lift--dur-3 · 160ms

Split-flap board Settled

Design System

  • One canvas per letter, each flipping round a drum of characters, drawn with motion blur so fast flaps smear like film. Letters are Geist; the plate and windows take their colours from the theme.
  • Everything about it, from timing and blur to the plate and grain, is tuned in the Split-Flap Lab, which keeps a dark and a light version. split-flap.js runs the active ones.
  • On the work page it sits in a Matte housing and names the project on show, with a key for each project beside it.
  • On phones the board drops its side columns, runs edge to edge and enlarges the letters. With reduced motion it shows the last frame straight away.

Halftone edge Draft

  • One colour arrives in another through a halftone: dots of the new colour grow until they touch, then holes of the old colour shrink away. It spans the full width of the window. The footer of this page is one, from the page’s ground into Slate.
  • Drawn by halftone.js on a canvas, with WebGL when it’s available, from the active version in the Halftone Lab. It may drift and respond to the cursor. It holds still for reduced motion.
  • Two of the earlier work sections, with the transition between them, are under Legacy.

Static spark Draft

  • When the pointer comes close to a button, a small bolt jumps from the button’s edge to the pointer, and the button flinches brighter for 280ms. It never fires while the pointer is on the button, and only on devices with a fine pointer and without reduced motion.
  • Reach, life, jaggedness, glow and colours are set in the Button Lab with the nav buttons. It is … in the active version.

16

Rules

Settled

How to use the parts. These hold for every assembly.

Accessibility

  • Text on a body is at least 4.5:1 against it. Lamps, value rings and the focus ring are at least 3:1. The tokens meet this in both finishes.
  • Every control works from the keyboard. Tab reaches it; Space or Enter works keys and switches; arrows move knobs, banks, segmented controls and tabs.
  • A lamp never carries meaning alone. Pair it with words.
  • The speckle in a plain key’s paint is iron: it stands up to the pointer and is thrown back by a press. It is decoration under the legend, never the only sign of anything, and it is switched off for reduced motion. Coloured, quiet, pad and disabled keys have none; data-plain turns it off on one key.
  • A control with no visible text needs an aria-label.
  • Targets are at least 24px. The tick box is 18px, so always wrap it in its label.
  • The focus ring appears at once and is never animated.

Content

  • A legend is one or two words with no punctuation. Write it in sentence case; the stylesheet sets the capitals.
  • A key says what it does: a verb, or a verb and a noun. “Save preset”, not “OK”.
  • Units follow the value in a dim legend: Hz, dB.
  • Numbers on a display use tabular figures and a true minus sign.
  • An error says what is wrong and what to do about it.
  • Running text is sentence case in the text face. Never set a sentence in capitals.

Layout

  • A body is a row of cells divided by seams. One job per cell.
  • The legend sits above its control; a state lamp sits below or beside it.
  • One primary key and one accent-filled part per body.
  • A body on the bench casts a shadow; a body inside a layout uses .m-panel--flat.
  • Below 480px a body marked .m-panel--reflow stacks its cells. Controls keep their size.
  • The light finish is a grey body, not a white one. Metal turns silver and meters show dark segments in a pale window; display glass stays black.

Icons

  • Lucide, the set the site already uses, at 12 or 16px with a 1.5 stroke, in the legend colour.
  • An icon replaces a legend only for actions everyone knows: previous, next, search, close, play.
  • An icon-only key needs an aria-label and a tooltip.

Naming

  • The site owns the scales: --space-*, --radius-*, --text-*, --dur-*, --ease-*, and raw colours as --c-*.
  • Matte tokens are roles, named --m-*, that point at those scales. A part never uses a raw value.
  • Classes are .m-part, .m-part__piece and .m-part--variant.
  • State lives in ARIA attributes (aria-pressed, aria-checked, aria-invalid), not in classes. The one exception is .is-on for a lamp.

Open issues

Known problems and undecided questions, to settle as each piece is.

  • Matte’s legends are smaller than most systems allow

    Printed legends are 10px and legends behind glass are 9px. Most systems keep working text at 11px or more. Either raise --m-text-legend and --m-text-glass, or keep the size and accept it for short capitals only.

  • Capitals

    Matte sets legends and keys in capitals. The phone cover’s plain controls had their capitals taken out as a preference. Decide whether that preference applies to Matte.

  • The cover’s control panel isn’t built from the system

    The Sound switch and tuning knob on the cover are their own drawing of a Matte panel. The Tuner under Assemblies is the same panel rebuilt from matte.css; the cover should use it. Until then the cover attaches the iron filings to its own knob and switch by hand.

  • Spike length on small parts

    The iron filings use one spike length for every part, so they stand tall on a small knob or a switch thumb. It could scale with the size of the face.

  • Some nav button colours aren’t in the palette

    The Matte version of the nav button carries its own greys (#2e2e30, #1f1f21, #b4b4b6 and the light finish’s) as plain values in the Button Lab. They match Matte’s tokens by eye, not by reference. Point them at --m-* tokens, or have the button take its colours from matte.css.

  • The type scale has near neighbours

    18, 17, 16 and 15px all appear. Intro (18) and body (17) could merge, and five of the text styles are no longer used by any page.
