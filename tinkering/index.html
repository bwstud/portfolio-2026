Tinkering

Experiments, sketches, and things I built to see what would happen.

  • Sandtable Acadia National Park as a glowing 3D particle cloud. Move the cursor to stir the sand. three.js · WebGL shaders
  • Old Glory A night-sky drone show of Americana. A thousand lights launch from the ground and hold a waving American flag. three.js · drone flight sim
  • Knot Dojo Ten essential knots, tied step by step in simulated rope. Pick a knot, a rope and a speed, and watch it from any angle. three.js · rope physics
  • Rocket Chess A chess set of famous spacecraft at 1:1250. Starship is the king, Saturn V the queen, and the pawns are satellites from Sputnik to Webb. three.js · scale models
  • GSAP Playground A sandbox for learning GSAP, one official skill at a time. Edit live code and scrub every tween on a timeline that draws its ease. GSAP · live code sandbox
  • Bonsai Raise a maple, elm or juniper from a nursery starter. Prune, pinch and wire it as it grows, then rewind the years and try again. three.js · tree growth model
  • Pottery wheel Throw a pot from a ball of clay. Centre, open, pull and shape it on a spinning wheel, then glaze it and fire it. three.js · clay that conserves its volume
  • Light desk Design a concert light show for ten famous American venues. Program cues on the beat, rebuild the rig, play the pads live, and bring your own song. three.js · GSAP · Web Audio
  • Chladni plate A steel plate covered in sand. Switch on the tone and turn the knob, and at each resonance the sand gathers on the lines where the plate holds still. three.js · Web Audio · plate modes
  • The Horse in Motion Muybridge's 1878 photographs of a galloping horse, cut from the card and run as a film. Slow it down to catch the moment all four hooves leave the ground. canvas · public-domain photographs
  • Taxidermy Museum-scanned skeletons of a giraffe and an extinct moa, every bone posable. Mount one on stone, wood or under glass, and take it home as a file for printing or CAD. three.js · inverse kinematics · photogrammetry
  • Bouquet Roses grown from a seed, every petal placed by botany, from bud to blown. Arrange them in a glass vase with a tape grid, and the stems lean, cross and settle as real ones do. three.js · position-based dynamics · procedural botany
