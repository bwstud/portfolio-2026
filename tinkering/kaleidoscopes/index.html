A kaleidoscope is two or three mirrors in a tube, reflecting whatever sits at the far end. Here the far end is a picture, and every pixel is folded back through the mirrors in a shader until it lands on the part of the picture the mirrors can see.

Drag to slide the picture under the mirrors, shift-drag to turn it, and scroll to magnify. Images reflected many times come out a little darker, as they do in real mirrors.

Photos and videos you add stay in your browser. Make new kaleidoscopes in the lab.
