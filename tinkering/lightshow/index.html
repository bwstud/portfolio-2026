Light deskdesign a concert light show

A lighting desk for concerts. Pick a venue and a song, and the default program runs straight away: a cue list on the beat grid, where each cue is a complete look for beams, washes, lasers, strobes, pixels and the screen.

Change any cue in the programmer, drag cues along the timeline, or play the pads live. The Rig tab rearranges the rig itself: move and reshape trusses, hang fixtures, and group them so a group can do something different in each cue. Uploaded songs are analysed for tempo and energy. YouTube and Spotify can't be heard by the page, so they follow the beat grid; tap the tempo in, or let the microphone listen.
