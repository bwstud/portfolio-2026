← Rigs and Jigs Oscilloscope

A simulated two-channel oscilloscope. The generators feed the inputs, and the scope draws what a beam would trace across the phosphor, dim where it moves fast and bright where it lingers.

Everything is recorded for the last five minutes, so you can pause, rewind and step through it frame by frame. Changing the timebase or trigger while rewound shows the same moment measured a different way. Changing a generator while rewound starts a new recording from that point.
