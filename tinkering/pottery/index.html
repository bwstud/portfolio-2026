A potter's wheel you can actually throw on. The clay is a stack of thin rings, and every tool conserves its volume: thin the wall and the clay rises, push it out and it gets thinner. Too wet, too thin or too much overhang and it slumps.

Every touch is a step you can undo or scrub back to. Save versions as you go, then cut it free, glaze it and fire it.
