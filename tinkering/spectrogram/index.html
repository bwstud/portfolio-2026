Drop an audio file to analyse it

A spectrogram shows a sound as a picture: time runs across, pitch runs up, and the colour at each point is how loud that pitch is at that moment. It is made by cutting the sound into short overlapping slices and taking the Fourier transform of each one.

Every setting of that transform is here. A long window sees pitch sharply but smears time; a short one does the opposite. Change it and the whole recording is analysed again. Pause and drag the picture to look back, and hover to read any point.
