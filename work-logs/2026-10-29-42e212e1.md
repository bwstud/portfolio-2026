### Piece mapping
All five major pieces share a true scale of **1:1250**.

| Piece | Craft | Real height |
|---|---|---|
| King | Starship (Block 3) | 124.4 m |
| Queen | Saturn V (Apollo 11 livery) | 110.6 m |

| Knight | Space Shuttle stack (Discovery) | 56.1 m |

Pawns are eight satellites in launch order, one per file a–h: Sputnik 1, Explorer 1, Telstar 1, Dong Fang Hong 1, Aryabhata, Voyager, Hubble, JWST. Each pawn is scaled so its largest dimension is 24 mm (ratio rounded to a "nice" number), on an 8 mm rod.

### Files created in `tinkering/rocket-chess/`
- **`kit.js`**: procedural modelling kit.
  - Geometry helpers: `revolve`, `loft` (with flat end caps), `slab`, `strut`, `bell`, `ogive`, `lattice`, `truss`.
  - A `Builder` that merges geometry by material.
  - Canvas-texture liveries and base materials.
- **`rockets.js`**: the five major models, built in real metres.
  - Stages, engines, fins, grid fins, escape towers, flaps and paint schemes.
  - The orbiter's black/white tiling uses per-vertex painting.
- **`satellites.js`**: the eight pawn models.
- **`sets.js`**: the `REAL` set, with per-piece metadata, specs and modelling notes. Estimated details are flagged in the notes.
- **`rocketchess.js`**: scene, board, pieces, lineup view and UI.
  - Board: brushed-metal plates in a steel frame with engraved file and rank letters.
  - Lighting: RoomEnvironment lighting, ACES tone mapping, soft shadows.
  - Views and options: board view and size-lineup view with a height ruler, a True 1:1250 vs Tournament scale toggle (K 95, Q 85, B 72, N 62, R 56 mm), a flight-livery vs ivory/graphite finish toggle, a turntable option, click-to-focus, and STL export in millimetres.
  - Test hook: exposes `window.__rc`.
- **`index.html`**: page shell reusing Knot Dojo CSS. The Fictional set is shown disabled with a "soon" tag.
- **`thumb.jpg`**: 960×600 card thumbnail.

## Challenges and how we solved them
- **Usage limit hit mid-build.**
  - Symptom: the work was cut off before the page was ever run, and I reported it as unverified.
  - Fix: after the limit reset, I ran the page in headless Chrome (puppeteer-core) and fixed what broke.
- **User couldn't see the page.**
  - Cause: the card had never been added to `tinkering/index.html`.
  - Fix: added the card and a thumbnail.
- **Inside-out nose cones.**
  - Symptom: pointed noses flared open like cups, and the Shuttle measured about 61 m.
  - Cause: the ogive radius used `(L - x)` instead of `x`.
  - Fix: corrected the formula in `kit.js`. Bounding boxes now measure 124.4, 110.6, 105.3, 56.1–56.2 and 46.3 m.
- **Review-caught model bugs, all fixed:**
  - Missing strut radius in the Saturn LES truss.
  - Self-intersecting forward-flap outline.
  - Escape-motor nozzles tilted inward.
  - F-1 manifold radius wrong.
  - Fairing disc covering the bells.
  - Closed SRB nozzle.
  - OMS pod scaled on the wrong axis.
  - N1 cable tunnel floating off the body.
  - Sputnik mount sign wrong.
  - Telstar sphere with 7 rows instead of 6.
- **Visual problems:**
  - Overexposed board and plinths: lowered env-map intensity.
  - Dark Starship steel: lightened it.
  - Jagged Shuttle paint boundary: smoothed the boundary function.
  - JWST mirror segments rotated 30° with gaps: removed the stray rotation.
  - Aryabhata too dark: brightened the cell colour.
  - Voyager boom invisible: thickened it.
  - Lineup guides misplaced by perspective: placed at z=0.
- **Phone layout.**
  - Symptom: the board was cropped and the a and h files were cut off. The lineup labels overlapped.
  - Fix: the camera distance now fits narrow screens, and the labels wrap onto two lines in smaller type.

## Lessons learned
- Always run a generated page in a real browser before reporting it done. The nose-cone bug and the missing index card were both invisible to a syntax check.
- Measure model bounding boxes against the published dimensions. It caught the ogive error immediately.
- The headless harness is `puppeteer-core` driving local Chrome, via `run.mjs` in the scratchpad. Usage: `node run.mjs "<query>" out.png [w h waitMs]`.
- The page's URL params are `ui=0`, `view=lineup`, `piece=<key>` and `shot`, and it exposes `window.__rc`.
- Keep estimated details flagged in each piece's "Model notes."
- Commit or push only when the user asks. If on the default branch, branch first.
- Don't touch `.env.local`.
