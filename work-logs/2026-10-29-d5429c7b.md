**Pass 2: tightening, lifts, realism**
- **Just-in-time lifts:** `tracks.js` now outputs a flat path plus `lift`/`lifts` data. `player.js` applies each lift via `smooth(at-0.1, at-0.035, moverProgress)` and shows a hand marker. Lifts begin about 0.8 s before the end reaches the crossing.
- **Full tightening:** hands keep drawing apart until the knot jams, then stop together (coupled stall logic) and hold tension. Pull distances are capped short of the table edge. Camera `follow()` tracks a contact-based "knot body". Two half hitches and round turn were authored about 5 cm closer to the rail so they seat.

## Challenges and how we solved them
- **Square knot spilled when pulled:** the σ²σ⁻² two-twist design was topologically trivial. Rebuilt as interlocked bights and verified with rope-to-rope contact counts.
- **Spread-based hold metric was meaningless:** replaced with segment-to-segment distance contact counts, and added a knot-size metric.
- **Regressions after the tightening rewrite (0 contacts = spilled):** bisected over many variants, found that finer SEG and the new bending model were the cause, reverted them, and got tightness from pull-until-jammed hands instead. Remaining slick-nylon failures were fixed by friction 0.3.
- **Black rope with AO on:** looked like a tuning problem but was a pipeline bug (inside-out tube winding). Fixing the winding fixed it.
- **AgX made reds salmon:** switched to Neutral tone mapping.
- **Rail call passed a texture instead of a filename:** fixed.
- **Slow playback in headless Chrome:** the committed version is equally slow, so it is the software renderer rather than the changes.
- **Failed push ("hung up"):** caused by the 2.5 GB of recordings in the commit, not the 4.8 MB of assets.
- **Headless Chrome limits:** minimum width about 500 px, iframe rAF throttled, Chrome extension not connected. Used top-level DevTools-protocol scripts instead.

## Lessons learned
- Check `git status` before committing, because the user drops large files into project folders mid-session. Stage by explicit path.
- Rope sim stability trades off against stretch: finer segments and softer bending cause stretch and slipping. Get tightness from the pull logic.
- Stop all grips together on stall so a tail can't be dragged back through the knot.
- Test the sim headless across materials and thicknesses (`hold.mjs`) before judging visuals.
- Use a fresh `--user-data-dir` per headless shot and a quoted heredoc (`<<'EOF'`) when generating scripts that contain `${...}`.
- The user wants plain-language explanations of rendering strategy and prefers that unrelated work (Rocket Chess) stay out of commits. The user authorised commit, PR and merge in one go ("Ship it").
