# Shipped Bonsai to production, with setup tabs and wire warnings

## Project Context
Bonsai is an experiment in the portfolio's tinkering section: you raise a bonsai, shape it with real techniques, and rewind time to try other choices. This session finished two requested features and shipped the project to GitHub and Vercel.

## Work Performed
- **Tabbed setup panel.** "Start a tree" now has Species, Setting, Container and Stand tabs, so the stand can be chosen before growing.
  - A summary line under the title keeps all four choices visible whichever tab is open.
  - Each stand card has a one-line description.
  - Until a stand is picked explicitly, the setting supplies its default. An explicit choice then sticks across settings.
  - Setup choices carry into the Display tab once growing starts.
- **Remove all wires** in Tree → Care, with a count of wires on.
  - It confirms first if any bend hasn't fully set and would spring back.
  - It's one undoable timeline entry: the model's unwire op now takes one wire, a list, or all.
- **Bite warnings.**
  - `sim.js` exports `wireTightness()`, the branch's swelling against the wire as a share of the bite threshold.
  - The coil warms from copper to red as tightness passes 0.6.
  - At 0.7 ("tightening") and again at the bite, growth pauses once per wire. A banner above the timeline explains the state and spring-back, with Show me, Remove it/them and Leave it/them on.
  - Warnings re-arm after a rewind. The inspector shows "Tightening" or "Biting in" with guidance. Guy wires never warn.
  - Tested in Node: a wire passed 0.7 about a year after wiring, a season before biting.
- **Shipped.**
  - Committed `d3a3ae7` (the first prototype) and `baac2e7` (winter garden, 500-year lifespan, light and display panels, stands, setup tabs, wire tools).
  - GitHub had a GSAP Playground pushed from the other laptop. I rebased both commits onto it and resolved the `tinkering/index.html` conflict so both cards are kept.
  - Pushed to `bwstud/portfolio-2026` main; Vercel deployed production automatically.
  - Verified on https://portfolio-2026-three-ochre.vercel.app: all Bonsai files and the new HDRI return 200; the tinkering index lists all six projects; the live page loaded in headless Chrome, its worker grew a juniper to age 8, and the camera framed it.

## Key Decisions
- Rebased rather than merged, so the two unpushed commits land on top of the remote's merge commit and history stays linear.
- Auto-pause fires once per wire per state, so playback isn't interrupted repeatedly by the same wire.

## Milestone
Bonsai is live in production: growth model, rewind and forking, three species, four settings, four containers, six stands, full lighting control, and wire care with warnings.
