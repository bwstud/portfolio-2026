# Light desk: a concert light show playground across ten US venues

## Project Context
A new tinkering project and motion design showcase: design a concert light show on a beat-grid cue list, in one of ten modelled American venues, to the built-in track, an uploaded file, or a YouTube or Spotify link. Versions and sessions persist, like the Bonsai and Pottery projects.

## Work Performed
- **Show engine (`show.js`)**:
  - A program is cues on a beat grid, and each cue is a complete look.
  - Fixture output is a pure function of program, beat and audio, so seeking and loading are deterministic.
  - Cue fades evaluate both looks and mix their outputs, with GSAP eases as fade shapes.
  - Effects keep moving while paused, so a held cue still animates during editing.
  - Includes a hand-built program for the demo track and an automatic program generator that uses an energy profile when one exists.
- **Look schema (`data.js`)**:
  - Seven groups (beams, washes, lasers, strobes, pixels, screen, room) defined as data, so the programmer UI is generated from it.
  - Twelve preset looks and ten palettes.
  - Facts and stage dimensions for the ten venues.
- **Rig (`rig.js`)**:
  - Instanced volumetric beam cones with a haze-noise shader and a near-camera fade.
  - Floor pools found by ray-marching against each venue's ground function.
  - Lens glow, laser fans, strobes and blinders, pixel strips, box trusses.
  - Deck, drum riser, band silhouettes and amps.
  - An LED-wall shader with seven visuals and A/B crossfade, also used for the Sphere's dome, MSG's ribbon boards, SoFi's halo screen and the ACL sky.
  - Seven real lights carry group averages onto the architecture.
- **Venues (`venues.js`)**, stylised and procedural:
  - Red Rocks: monoliths, steep rows, Denver's lights.
  - Hollywood Bowl: concentric shell tinted by the washes.
  - MSG: bowl, spoked ceiling, bridges, ribbon boards.
  - Sphere: the dome plays the visuals.
  - Ryman: pews, horseshoe balcony, stained glass.
  - Radio City: telescoping arches with cove lights, mezzanines, gold curtain.
  - The Gorge: lawn, canyon, river, dusk sky.
  - SoFi: stands, translucent roof, Infinity Screen.
  - Fillmore: damask walls, chandeliers, balcony.
  - ACL Live: tiered balconies, skyline backdrop.
  - Plus an instanced crowd that bobs to the beat, with phone lights in quiet passages.
- **Audio (`audio.js`)**:
  - "Afterglow", an original 96-bar deep house track synthesised offline with about 50 automated nodes.
  - Transports for the demo buffer, uploaded audio or video, the YouTube IFrame API, the Spotify Embed API, and silence.
  - Analyser bands with onset detection.
  - Tempo, downbeat and per-bar energy detection for uploads.
  - Microphone listening for embeds.
- **App and UI (`app.js`, `index.html`)**:
  - Setup card, camera shot bar (auto director, six shots, free orbit), and programmer tabs (Cue, Show, Versions).
  - Dock: transport, BPM with tap tempo, hold pads (flash, strobe, blackout, lasers), and a timeline. Cues drag by bar or beat, click to seek, double-click to add; the timeline shows an energy lane.
  - Undo and redo, session restore, named versions with colour strips, JSON export and import.
  - Show mode and a GSAP title card.
  - Post-processing: a NaN and HDR guard, bloom, Neutral tone mapping, vignette, grain and slight chromatic aberration.
  - Phone layout with a bottom sheet.
- Gallery card and thumbnail added to `tinkering/index.html`.
- Verified in headless Chrome (SwiftShader):
  - All ten venues across several shots.
  - Demo playback; timeline click, drag and undo; presets, palette, add cue; version save; strobe pad.
  - YouTube link load and playback: title, length, auto-program, clock advancing.
  - Upload detection on a generated 128 bpm file: found 128 bpm and a 0.308 s downbeat (true 0.31 s).
  - Session restore; 390 px layout.
  - The console showed only the site-wide favicon 404 and gesture-gated audio warnings.

## Key Decisions
- Deterministic show function rather than live tweened state: scrubbing, versions and seeks always reproduce the show. GSAP handles fades, the camera and the title.
- YouTube and Spotify can't be analysed in the page, so they follow the beat grid, with tap tempo and an optional mic. Uploads and the demo get real analysis.
- The demo track is synthesised in the browser, so the out-of-box show has real audio reactivity without shipping or licensing audio.
- Shots lift themselves above whatever ground is underfoot, and a guard pass removes NaN and extreme pixels before bloom; together they fixed black frames and square blotches.
