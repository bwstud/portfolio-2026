## Challenges and how we solved them
- **Stems tunnelled through the glass under the lip.** Segments are 2.5–3.5 cm long, so a segment could cross the wall between two particles. Fix: a crossing check that lifts the segment over the lip.
- **Stems were 3× too floppy.** Gauss-Seidel only carries stiffness one particle per iteration. Fix: multi-span bending stencils, 32 substeps and 3.5 cm segments. The result converges on beam theory.
- **The sim never slept.**
  - A bug slept it on a timer instead.
  - Its jitter came from a 0.5 g cut-end particle under about 35 g of stem and head (mass ratio).
  - Fixes: a 6 g floor on the cut end, contacts solved before and after the stem constraints, settling damping after 1 s, and a sleep threshold of 3.5 mm/s.
- **New stems launched others out of the vase.** Fixes: a per-substep push cap (0.6 mm), the placement search, and an exact wall intersection in `placeStem` (the old loop could leave the cut end outside).
- **Stems wedged mid-grid.** Friction of 0.6 was too high for wet glass. Fix: separate friction per surface.
- **The rose looked like a peony or camellia.** Fixes: the opening schedule, bloom-scaled reach and reflex, a tighter open cup, lower ruffle, and sepals moved outside the petals.
- **The red was candy-pink.** Fix: a deeper face colour and less sheen.
- **The water smeared the stems.** The transmission thickness was the full diameter. Fix: 2.6 cm.
- **Syntax error:** `-(x) ** 2` isn't allowed in JS.

## Unsolved problems
- **Rose shape:** the bud is a little blunt rather than pointed. Half open is still slightly wide for a hybrid tea.
- **Leaf collisions:** leaves don't collide, so they can pass through other stems.
- **Slow creep:** some contact creep (about 4 mm/s) remains on heavily loaded stems until sleep. It's a limit of position-based friction.
- **Twist:** the twist ("Face") is cosmetic. The rod has no torsion.
- **Glass and water:** the glass is a blended shell, not refraction. The thick base is transmissive, so it isn't seen through the water from above.
