## Work done
- **`tinkering/fold-dojo/engine.js`:** a flat-folding engine. The sheet is a list of convex facets, each with its outline in paper coordinates and a 2D affine placement. A fold step is a set of moves. Each move picks facets by side of a line, layer from the top, part of the sheet or a tag, and turns them about a chain of hinges. One hinge is a valley or mountain fold. Two nested hinges give a petal fold. Inside-reverse folds open the layers like a book and slip between the layers they land among. Also flips, turns, unfolds (the previous fold played backward, with its creases kept) and custom moves. Stacking order after each fold comes from comparing, for every overlapping pair, which way the layers were travelling as they landed: outer hinge first, then inner, then starting layer, sorted topologically. Shaping steps (wings spread) keep a 3D pose, and a presentation turn stands the model up.
- **`designs.js`:** crane (30 steps), paper airplane (11, on A4, A5 or Letter, with geometry from the sheet's proportions), samurai helmet (9), heart (13), cup (5). The crane's square base is a real rigid collapse: the eight triangles round the centre turn about their creases, rise into a four-pointed star, then flatten.
- **`paper.js`:** 18 colours and 14 patterns, all drawn in canvas: plain, washi, waves (seigaiha), hemp leaf (asanoha), blossom, dots, stripes, gingham, marbled (suminagashi), rainbow, kraft, newsprint, gold and silver foil. The other side can be white, the same or a second colour. Five weights from 50 to 160 gsm change thickness (drawn at 1.6× real), how much a flap curls as it moves, roughness and how strongly creases show. Square sizes are 7.5, 15, 24 and 35 cm. Creases are drawn onto the texture as each fold adds one.
- **`surfaces.js`:** desk (Poly Haven wood scan), concrete counter (Concrete Floor 02 scan, downloaded this session), cutting mat (drawn: cm grid, angle guides, numbers), rubber pad on the desk, glass table (transmission, steel frame, wooden floor below, shadow catcher).
- **`app.js` and `index.html`:** studio HDR lighting, a key light with shadows, and optional contact shadows (GTAO). **Watch** mode has transport, speed, step bar and keyboard shortcuts. **Fold it yourself** mode: the part that moves glows, amber dashes mark a valley fold and blue dots a mountain fold. You grab the paper and drag. The step follows the grabbed point's path on screen, and releasing past 45% finishes the fold. Grabbing a part that doesn't move explains why. Hint escalates and Show me plays the step. Undo is there too. Camera views (front, top, side, low), a zoom slider and follow-the-paper. The **viewer** offers six HDR settings, a plinth, the floor (grounded skybox) or floating, background blur and a turntable. Flat models stand upright in it. **Saved pieces** store design, paper settings and a thumbnail in localStorage, and reopen in the viewer. All labels are sentence case, with no uppercase styling.
- **Assets:** five HDRs and the wood scans copied from Pottery, Bonsai and Knot Dojo; concrete scans downloaded from Poly Haven (CC0). `assets/CREDITS.md` lists them all. About 13 MB in total.
- **Gallery:** Fold Dojo card added to `tinkering/index.html` after Bouquet. `thumb.jpg` (960×600) is the finished crane on the desk.

## Challenges and how we solved them
- **Collapse ties:** in the square base, the two triangles of each pressed flap coincide exactly, so their order came out arbitrary and the wrong quadrant ended up in front. Fixed with a tie-break: the triangle nearer the front flap's corner goes in front.
- **White wings:** the first petal fold tucked the sides under the rising flap, so the finished crane had white wings on top. Reversing the side creases (the sides swing in and cover the flap) gives a coloured bird base and coloured wings.
- **Helmet horns:** the horn fold's region took in the bottom half too. Fixed with a boundary at the base of the horns.
- **Neck and tail hidden:** the first reverse-fold angle left both inside the wing outline. Re-angled to send the legs out at 134° so they show.
- **Airplane upside down:** the presentation turn had the wrong sign.
- **Camera framed far out:** it framed the sheet before its scale was applied. Fixed by updating the matrices first.
- **Testing:** each design was checked in Node first with top-down SVG drawings of every step, then in headless Chrome (Playwright, CPU rendering) with screenshots, a scripted drag and a save-and-reopen run.

## Unsolved problems
- Seen only in headless Chrome, which renders on the CPU. Real-time playback there was slow, because the frame step is capped at 50 ms. Frame rate, GTAO cost and glass transmission on a real GPU are not measured.
- Not tried by hand in a browser: drag feel, touch on a phone, and orbit and pan alongside the paper grab.
- Simplifications: layers don't bend round each other at creases; inside-reverse and petal folds move rigidly, so hidden layers can briefly separate mid-move; the cup's front flap folds over the front instead of tucking into the pocket; the crane's body isn't puffed out.
- Two viewer settings looked plain in testing (Japanese house behind the plinth, the floor option). Worth judging on a real screen.
