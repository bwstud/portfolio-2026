## Work done

### Chladni cover (first `lab/hook.html`, then `lab/hook/chladni.html`, now `index.html`)
- A flat 2D rewrite of the plate, not the three.js lab project. Up to about 120,000 grains simulated on the CPU and drawn as WebGL points. The field is a blend of two Chladni figures, cos(nπu)cos(mπv) ± cos(mπu)cos(nπv), sampled on a 4px grid; grains slide down the slope of its square and hop where the plate moves. A "rest" band near the nodal lines gives the sand width instead of hairlines.
- The plate is as wide as the page's longer side, so the page shows the middle band and every figure is symmetric about the centre.
- One frequency value drives everything. Ten figures sit on a pentatonic scale (A major pentatonic across two octaves) `[220, 247.5, 277.2, 330, 370, 440, 495, 554.4, 660, 740]` Hz, chosen for a pleasant tone, not physical resonances. Left alone, the frequency drifts to a neighbouring note every 4.8s with a doubled smootherstep, so it crawls as a figure forms and runs in between. It opens on a random figure.
- Cursor parts the grains (130px). Click sends a ring (strength 2.9, up from 2.2). Displaced grains live in a separate "offset" channel with a spring, so they heal over about two seconds.
- Sentence arrives word by word, centred; "I'm Brian." 42px in Ochre, sentence 68px. The sand fades only behind the sentence.
- Controls panel: slide switch for sound (label "Sound" in Söhne Mono, a lamp and a fixed "On"; off by default; Space toggles), tuning knob ported from the lab (drag, scroll or arrow keys), Hz readout animated with NumberFlow 0.6.2 from jsDelivr (at most about 11 updates a second, 360ms roll, plain-number fallback), previous/next jump buttons (`[` and `]`) that glide to the neighbouring figure. Touching the knob or a jump stops the drift; it resumes 3.5s later. Sound is a sine tone following the frequency plus faint sand hiss.
- `?ff=<seconds>` fast-forwards the simulation for screenshots (`&pulse=x,y,age`, `&mouse=x,y`, `&gather=±s`).

### Recent Work hover and exit (current state)
- Hover or keyboard focus on Recent Work gathers every grain under the key; the cursor push is off while over it. **Gather is now a fixed 100ms run** (replacing the spring): each grain runs straight to its spot, arriving between 60 and 100ms; the key lights 100ms after the pointer arrives (was 220ms). Leaving springs the grains back over about 1.5s. Reduced motion keeps the plain highlight.
- **The key is a Matte key** (44px tall, drawn arrow). Because it is opaque, grains now rush in under the key and the key lights in the sand colour instead of the sand visibly filling an outline.
- **Pitch freezes** while the key is hovered or focused, or the cover is leaving: the drift clock stops, so the Hz number, the knob dot, the copper ring and the scale dot freeze together; the drift resumes from where it stopped. Sound holds pitch too. Untested.
- **Exit on press (current):** the Arrival-glyph exit was built over several rounds and then **dropped at the user's request**; all glyph code is removed. Now: the sand vanishes on the same frame as the press (no fade), the sentence and the Recent Work key rise and fade, and the control panel **dissolves into particles** (see Dissolve below). The cover opens `work.html` when the dissolve finishes, so a longer or shorter version needs no other change. Cmd-click and new-tab clicks open the page normally; reduced motion makes the link a plain link; a cross-fade view transition is used where supported. Back-to-cover restore is untested.
- Work page arrival when coming from the cover: nav fades in, the work rises 160ms later (the name/tagline step was removed along with the name and tagline).

### Knob synchronisation (cover panel)
- The white dot, the end of the copper ring and the lit scale dot now share one angle. Previously the dot turned a full circle per octave (two turns across the range) while the ring swept the range once over a 300° arc. The scale now has 12 equally spaced dots (one per figure plus one at each end of the range, 200 and 800 Hz); because the figures aren't evenly spaced in pitch, the knob moves in equal angular steps from one figure to the next. Dragging now keeps the dot under the pointer, so the full range is one 300° sweep, about 2.4 times faster per degree than before. Arrow keys and the wheel make the same pitch steps. `ARC = 300, R = 44, R_TICK = 54, C = 58`. Verified in stills at three pitches only.

## Challenges and how we solved them

- **Blank canvas on first run.** `requestAnimationFrame` can report a time slightly before `performance.now()`, making the figure index −1. Clamped elapsed time at zero.
- **No way to drive a browser.** The Chrome extension wasn't connected, so everything was checked with headless Chrome screenshots. Virtual time barely advances a heavy loop, hence `?ff`. Headless resizes the window once after load, which re-seeded the grains; resize now rescales positions and the fast-forward re-runs at the final size (and sets `freq = NOTES[3]` on re-run to avoid a NaN).
- **Sand collapsed to hairlines and sparse figures.** Added the rest band and base jitter; switched to higher modes.
- **Pulse cleared the whole screen.** Lowered strength and moved pushes to the healing offset channel.
- **Hover fill came out as a fuzzy blob.** Gathered grains now track their on-screen position directly; later the spring was replaced with a fixed 100ms timed run.
- **110px text didn't fit 1440×900.** Resolved when the user chose 42px / 68px.
- **Cymascope cover "not working" (timings, Fill screen, only "Copy settings" visible, then an empty ring).** The plain `python3 -m http.server` let the browser cache files, so the user ran an old `app.js` without `apply`; the cover waited forever. While in that state the first version auto-saved `drive: 0`, which later loaded as an empty dish. Fixes: a no-cache server (port 8091), versioned script addresses, a fallback when `apply` is missing, and dropping a zero drive on load. Strobe drift in the hidden playback bar was drowning the 9s pulse, so the bar was restored in tuning mode. Inferred, not inspected on their browser.
- **Opacity would have faded the control panel too** if applied to the iframe; applied to the canvas inside.
- **Synthetic keydown on window threw** `e.target.closest is not a function` → dispatched on `document.body` and used `closest?.`.
- **Matte Lab:** tab panels all showed (`.m-cell { display: grid }` beat `hidden`) → `.matte [hidden] { display: none !important; }`; popover/dialog didn't hide (`.m-panel` display rule beat UA rules) → `:not(:popover-open)` / `:not([open])` rules; the Tier 1 refactor script crashed on a global replace order, fixed with a temporary placeholder; contrast failures found by script and fixed (see above).
- **Phone-width screenshots** couldn't go below about 500px, so narrow layouts remain unchecked.
- **Arrival glyph exit** (built, then dropped): iterations on count (×10), speed (100→400ms), outward vs backwards flight; the user couldn't get it to read as flying away from the viewer and dropped the idea. Lesson: stills at fixed times didn't prove the depth read; the user judged by eye.
- **Dissolve "exploding sand":** pressing the key released the grains from under it so they sprang back to the figure while fading; fixed by hiding the sand on the press frame.
- **Dissolve took too long to show:** the saved version's curve of 8 delays visible bites; explained, not changed.
- **Headless stills went blank after adding the pulse.** A late `resize` cleared the canvas of a held frame. Held frames now redraw on resize.
- **New root files 404 on the dev server.** `tools/lab-server.mjs` reads `.vercelignore` at start-up and serves only what ships, so a new allowlisted file needs a server restart.
- **The lit lamp was overridden by the unlit rule** (lower specificity). The lit selector now carries the same `:not()` parts.
- **Text sliding in from the left was cut off** because `main { overflow-x: clip }` clips at the text's own edge. Fixed with a negative margin and equal padding on `main`.
- **The Matte Lab seemed to disappear** when the Rigs and Jigs nav was rebuilt from the work page's, whose Controls menu never listed it. Links restored on both pages.

## Lessons learned

- Serve prototypes with `Cache-Control: no-store`. Stale files cost several rounds of confusion.
- The user iterates in small visual steps and judges by eye and ear; give the address, say what was and wasn't verified, and keep moving. They will try timings by running numbers ("try 200ms", "300ms", "400ms") and drop an idea quickly if it isn't reading.
- The user prefers the Cymascope's own controls over duplicates on the cover; prefers lab pages with sliders and saved versions for anything they will tune by eye (Dissolve Lab, Button Lab).
- `/pick-ui-library` picks from Emil Kowalski's list: NumberFlow fit the Hz readout; the switch redesign wasn't covered and was done by hand in CSS.
- Saving state automatically while a connection is broken can persist bad values; the cover now saves only on explicit action.
- The user responds strongly to reference-image-driven restyles and escalates quickly from a restyle to a system; keep parts derived from tokens so tone, accent and finish apply everywhere. They then propagate it across the site (cover buttons, work page, Button Lab).
- Contrast needs checking numerically; the pale finish failed several pairs that looked fine by eye. "White" hardware needs a mid grey body to show shadow and speckle.
- Frozen-frame screenshots cannot confirm timing or depth of motion; say so and let the user judge.
