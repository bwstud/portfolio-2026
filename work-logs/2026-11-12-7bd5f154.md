**Chladni cover on phones** (`index.html`)
- Type: `clamp(26px, min(8.4vw, 7.6svh), 68px)`; the name is `.62em` of it. Desktop is unchanged at 68px / 42px.
- `viewport-fit=cover` and safe-area padding.
- ≤600px wide: the controls dock shrinks with `zoom: .82` (keeps the knob's pointer maths true). ≤380px: the Hz readout hides.
- Landscape under 520px tall: the dock (`zoom: .66`) and Recent Work sit side by side under the sentence.
- Touch: a dragging finger parts the sand like the cursor (radius capped at 24% of the short side); a tap sends the pulse on lift if the finger moved under 24px, matching where `boom.js` plays its sound. A mouse still pulses on press.
- Covers menu (`cover/covers.js`): at ≤600px wide or ≤520px tall it sits top right and opens downward.

**Phone nav menu** (`nav.js`, `styles.css`, `work.html`)
- ≤600px: one 60px row. The first button (Work) on the left; Menu, Controls and the theme switch on the right.
- `nav.js` builds the Menu panel by cloning the page's link chips, so every page with the shared nav gets it with no markup changes. The panel is full-width 48px rows; the icon turns into a cross; Esc, tapping outside, picking a link or opening Controls closes it; reduced motion skips the fade.
- Removed `work.html`'s phone rule that flowed all buttons as one wrapping run.

## Challenges and how we solved them

- **Pull failed with "Repository not found".** Wrong active `gh` account. The first `gh auth switch` was blocked by the permission classifier until the user asked for it explicitly.
- **Headless Chrome screenshots at 390px weren't phone-sized.** The cover's own ≤480px rule didn't apply in them. Switched to Playwright 1.63 (installed in the session scratchpad, driving the system Chrome via `channel: 'chrome'`) with the iPhone 13 device profile: a true 390×664 viewport, touch and 3× density.
