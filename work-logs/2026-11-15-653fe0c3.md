### Chain Reaction (`tinkering/rube-goldberg/`)
- `params.js`: about 95 settings in 13 sections, one per link plus room and playback, and the default layout.
- `geom.js`: shapes the sim and the drawing share: the jump profile (inrun, circular transition, table, smoothstep landing hill, outrun, wall), the operator's tip-back maths and lens point, and the whale's cross-sections.
- `sim.js`: bakes the whole run in one go, deterministic, at 480 Hz, recorded at 60 fps (about 75 ms in Node). Each link is its own physics, and any link can fail with a reason ("Lens stopped 89 cm short of the pad"):
  - skier: slides along the profile, then flies with lift and drag;
  - operator: an inverted pendulum, leaning on the wall;
  - lens: tumbles, bounces and rolls;
  - spring pad: launches the lens, aimed at the cube (it can solve the speed to reach it);
  - gravity cube: a spring, damping and a speed-squared "syrup", and it turns the lens to face the laser;
  - laser: heats the pile, scaled by focus against the lens's focal length;
  - powder trail: burns along a Catmull-Rom curve;
  - blast: rigid chunks, the head and tail pieces, 1,500 mist particles bouncing off walls, floor and ceiling, and up to 500 splatter decals.
- `build.js`: everything is procedural:
  - concrete floor with saw-cut joints, block walls, LED fixtures;
  - timber ski jump with scaffold and snow sparkle, padded wall;
  - posable skier (stand, tuck, V-style flight, telemark), camera operator with a shoulder camera;
  - lens, spring pad, shader gravity cube, laser on a tripod, gunpowder pile, a powder trail whose shader burns it;
  - whale in three pieces, plus chunk shapes.
- `fx.js`: all effects are drawn from playback time, so they rewind exactly: fire and smoke bursts, the mist, instanced splatter decals, the beam.
- `audio.js`: synthesised sounds for each event, plus beds for snow hiss, wind, fuse, cube hum and laser.
- `app.js` and `index.html`:
  - a chain panel with each link's status and the reason a run broke;
  - a tuning panel where every number has a slider plus a typed field, per the project's tuning-lab rule, with per-row and per-section reset;
  - click to select, then move or turn with the gizmo. Position fields are kept in sync both ways. The operator is stored relative to the wall, so he moves with the jump;
  - versions in localStorage, with export and import as JSON. The working state autosaves;
  - transport: start, rewind (1–8×), frame back, play, frame forward, fast forward (2–8×), slow motion, and a scrubber with event marks;
  - camera: orbit, fly (WASD, Q and E), follow the action, Reset camera, and blast shake.
- Added a card on `tinkering/index.html` and `thumb.jpg` (960 × 600, the blast).
- Tuned the defaults in Node so the whole chain fires: lands 1.27 m past the edge, hits the wall at 1.07 m/s, lens in the cube at 5.2 s and at rest at 10.8 s, laser at 11.4 s, pile at 11.9 s, blast at 17.2 s, run 26.2 s.

## Challenges and how we solved them
- **Rewind and frame stepping need to be exact.** Live physics can't run backwards, so the sim bakes the whole run and playback only reads frames. Fire, smoke and mist age are computed from time in shaders.
- **Landing distance jumped between 0.5 m and 1.6 m with tiny pop changes.** The smootherstep hill was too flat at the knoll, so the jumper skimmed onto it. Switched it to smoothstep.
- **The whale looked hollow,** with its raw cut ends showing through. The skin triangles were wound inwards. Flipped the winding and shrank the end caps inside the skin.
- **Z-fighting** on the dado band and where the outrun meets the floor. Fixed by raising the near plane to 2 cm, lifting the jump 4 mm and offsetting the band.
- **Headless Playwright** couldn't find its expected browser build. Pointed it at the cached Chromium 1234.
- **`matte.css` came back 404 from the dev server** after the merge. The server reads the `.vercelignore` allowlist at start-up and had been started before the merge. Restarted it. `matte.css` is on the allowlist, so production is unaffected.

- **The bison page still drove itself** after the cursor was removed. Replaced its clock with the hold driver. Its first rest frame was 6% exploded, so it now picks the stillest standing frame.
- **Code-sculpted raptors never reached photo-painterly.** The feathered version's feathers read as leaves, and the sculpted one as a newt. I said so, and the user chose to generate the paintings.
- **WebGL "feedback loop" error:** the shadow buffer was still bound to a texture unit the shader declares. Fixed by always binding a blank 1 × 1 texture in its place.
- **The cut-out kept the generator's grey shadow,** and the flood fill did nothing on an image made from a numpy array. Fixed by keying connected neutral greys as background and flood-filling a copy.
- **The painting's spine isn't its centre,** because the legs hang below. The tool takes the trunk depth from a low percentile and sets the spine from the back edge.
