## Challenges and how we solved them
- **Settle timing measured wrong at first.** Headless Chrome ran the 118k-grain simulation slowly, so the wall-clock numbers were inflated. The fix was to measure in simulated time with an injected step loop, against the quiet before the drift picks up.
- **The repeated-hop stall** came from `jump()` targeting from the current frequency mid-glide (the same note again) with a zero-velocity curve.
- **Headless screenshots on the design-system page** often missed the control, because that page's scrolling doesn't match the clip coordinates. I fell back to counting canvas pixels. Only the pads were seen in a screenshot.
- **Name clash:** `wired` already existed in `matte.js`; renamed mine to `ironed`.
- **The first pass at removing the face dot** counted faint stray pixels as marks. The assertion stopped it before saving, and the rerun used the threshold the analysis used.
- **The fader's centre line** kept about 50 iron pixels at its edges until the clear strip was widened half a pixel on each side.
