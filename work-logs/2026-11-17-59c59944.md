**The taste profile v0 as delivered in chat**
- Core read: the user likes interfaces that behave like well-made instruments. A quiet, dark, solid body holds one living thing that follows a real physical model. Controls feel physical, motion responds instantly then settles gracefully, type borrows from technical documents, and everything else stays restrained.
- Six traditions in the collection: design engineering (Emil Kowalski, Vercel, Josh Puckett, Diego Haz); creative coding and WebGL; the Braun / Teenage Engineering line of instrument design; technical drawings and data printouts; ink, paper and East Asian calm; sites that follow time of day.
- Nine traits:
  1. Physical truth over decoration.
  2. Controls as hardware.
  3. One signature object, with everything else quiet.
  4. "Graceful, yet very fast", matching the tokens (responses 60–320 ms with a strong ease-out, shorter exits, slow background motion).
  5. Images built from a grid of dots or cells (halftone, ASCII, LED cards, Perler beads, voxels, split-flap).
  6. Technical-document type: Söhne, Söhne Breit and Söhne Mono.
  7. A dark cool ground with one warm accent, and paper or linen tones for light mode.
  8. AI that shows its work.
  9. A stage with its control panel beside it.
- Probable dislikes, to be confirmed: generic SaaS gradients, gold "premium" accents, decorative parallax or jitter, swirling particle-cloud entrances, full app screenshots as feature art, hidden scrollbars.

## Challenges and how we solved them

- **Tweet embeds opaque through the Notion connector.** The API returned them as "unknown" blocks without URL or text. The user's HTML export preserved the links, which gave 102 unique tweet URLs.
- **Chrome extension not connected.** Not fixed this session. The export-plus-fxtwitter route made it unnecessary for the tweets.
- **Python could not fetch HTTPS (no certificate store set up on this machine).** Switched the fetcher to `curl` and reran. Removed the empty files left by the failed run and refetched.

## Lessons learned

- A Notion HTML export preserves embed URLs that the connector drops.
- For motion-heavy collections, sampling video frames with ffmpeg onto labeled contact sheets works well and keeps the volume manageable.
- The user's corrections recorded in work logs are the strongest evidence of taste, stronger than saves alone.
- The user's one note in their own words was more informative than dozens of bare embeds. Asking them to add a line on why each piece was saved will help most.
- Keep durable artifacts out of the scratchpad.
