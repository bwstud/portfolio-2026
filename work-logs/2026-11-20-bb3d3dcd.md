**Tatankagram (built as "Bison Beads", renamed; committed 0ef5a62, shipped in PR #43)**

*Data pipeline — `tools/bison/tatankagram.py`* (was `beads.py`; run with `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b model/src/bison_blender.blend --python tatankagram.py` from `tools/bison`)
- Rests the 3DRT bison model and scatters points over the surface. Head set is a bust (head, beard, front of the hump), sampled with weighting toward surfaces facing the viewer (floor 0.5). Smooth normals blended across faces; a coat offset lifts beads off the skin (thicker in dark shag). Writes `head.bin` and `whole.bin`.
- Later changes: 60,000 beads, each with the coat's real colour and coat lift. Then the neck thins out and loosens: the head is sampled up to 2.4 m back, thinning past 0.6 m; past the jaw, beads scatter more with distance, drift while held, shrink and dim (15-byte records with a "looseness" value; about 17,600 of the 60,000 head beads are in the loose trail, files about 879 KB each). The whole animal is unaffected. README updated.

*Page — `tinkering/tatankagram/`* (`index.html`, `tatankagram.js`, `versions.js`, `thumb.jpg`; WebGL 2 renderer plus Matte panel)
- Shapes for the cloud between animals (picker "Shape between animals", crossfade of about 0.8–1 s): Smoke ring, Dust devil, Nebula, Murmuration, Galaxy, Prairie grass, Orbits, Globe. `?shape=` sets the starting shape. Orbits is now the default.
- Saved versions in the style of the other labs (Save, Open with this, Delete, Original protected); stores only values that differ from defaults. Store registered in `tools/lab-server.mjs` (`/__lab/tatankagram` → `tinkering/tatankagram/versions.js`, global `TATANKAGRAM_VERSIONS`).
- Transport: Play/pause, Restart (was Replay; now a true restart of loop, ring spin, scene rotation, camera and light, and it cancels a held bison), loop scrubber, and a form-toggle key ("Form the bison" / "Back to the shape") that plays the real gather or fall and holds, skipping waits; reduced motion jumps straight to the result.
- Beads panel: Number of beads 2,000–60,000 (default 30,000), Density (packed on the skin to loose and furry). New "Material and colour" panel: six materials (brushed metal, chrome, glass, pearl, clay, plastic) and three colour modes (one colour, dark to pale, the coat's own colours). The Warmth fader was removed. Stray pink and lilac beads in coat-colour mode were fixed in the shader (dark samples fade to neutral, saturation capped near 0.22, no bead bluer than green).
- Transition rework: orbit rings are three nested concentric rings (no collisions), each at its own tilt. Instead of wind, beads fall into their rings under gravity (speed up from rest, keep moving round the ring, small landing bounce). Each bead goes to the ring chosen by its distance from the centre and the nearest point on it, spread evenly round each ring. The fall applies to every shape. Loop is about half a second longer.
- Easing: "Forming the head" (gather) and "Forming the shape" (fall) each have a Bézier curve drawing, a preset menu (Linear … Anticipate and overshoot, "Custom" after manual edits), four handle faders (progress −0.6 to 1.6) and, for the fall, a Landing bounce fader. Defaults: gather Ease in and out, fall Gravity.
- Camera/view: default angle matched to the user's screenshot by rendering candidates (yaw 0.5, pitch −0.5), later softened. Camera panel: Turn (default 20°) and Tilt faders, a "Start from this view" key (captures just the camera), Reset view, Rotation speed (−30 to 30 °/s, default 4) and Sway. Tilt of the head runs from 12° to −16° ("Tilt by the release"), starting `Tilt lead` (0.5 s) before the head is fully formed. The tilt is applied to the head itself (uHeadTilt), not the camera, so the rings never tip. The head keeps its final tilt through the fall, and the tilt direction alternates each cycle so nothing ever jumps. Restart resets the count.
- Pointer effects removed entirely (brush and look-at-pointer, with their faders). Dragging now turns the animal; scroll zooms; cursor is a grab hand; hint reads "Drag to turn · Scroll to zoom".
- Light: the sun-arc idea was replaced by a tunable sweep. The sweep starts from the Light faders' rest position and makes full circles (no backward dip), from the first moment of the loop. Laps are tied to the loop clock: one lap per head phase (Gather + Hold) and one per ring phase (Blow away + In the dust), so the rule holds when timings change. Laps meet with the light behind the head (at gather start and at release) and pass in front mid-hold, worked out from the current timings. Controls: sweep on/off, Turns (laps per phase), Angle in front (−25°), Linger in front (0.6), Rise behind. Light angle is now only the rest position with the sweep off. Sweep time, From/To, height faders and Dim at the ends were removed. The light follows its path exactly while running and glides when the form key skips ahead. Measured head brightness across the hold: 69 → 84 → 44 (before: 50 → 84 → 42).
- Orbits panel: per ring (inner, middle, outer) Spin (83 / 57 / 41 °/s), Tumble (8 / −6 / 4.5 °/s), Starting tilt (20° / 60° / 36°), Starting turn (0° / 120° / −114°), twelve faders with typed fields. Rings run in real time with their own accumulated angles (per frame), so speeds don't jump and Dust speed (saved version "Tune 2" has 0.05) no longer scales them. A hidden drift was removed.
- Panel layout: spacing between script-built panels, Save button fits its panel, menus fill the panel width.
- `?` URL parameters: `?t=`, `?whole`, `?view=yaw,pitch`, `?shape=`.

## Challenges and how we solved them
- **Flat, hollow head:** the head set was half the animal seen dead-on without perspective, with sparse dark face beads. Fixed with view-facing weighted sampling, the bust crop, a three-quarter view with perspective, smooth normals, coat thickness and a raking light. Subdivision smoothing melted the head into a blob and was reverted.
- **Blender output location:** bead files landed in the wrong folder; moved and the path fixed.
- **Octahedral encoding, record-offset (`i*14` → `i*15`) and view-axis bugs** fixed during the builds.
- **`wake()` init-order error** after adding the materials: dropped the early call (`loadConfig` sets the look).
- **Screenshot tooling:** zsh didn't split a list of angles; each address had to be written out. Headless screenshots of temp HTML copies 404'd because the lab server only serves allowlisted files.
- **"Gather loop control doesn't change timing":** measured the head forming about 1 s at Gather 0.6 vs about 6 s at Gather 4, so the fader worked. Real problems: changing Gather while held left the clock mid-hold; pressing Back to the shape mid-gather played forward through the hold. Fixed: held state follows the setting; a mid-gather return runs the clock backward.
- **Ring spin/tumble apparently dead:** caused by scaling with Dust speed (0.05 in "Tune 2") and by angle = speed × time jumps; fixed with real-time per-frame accumulation.
- **Pink/lilac beads:** about 250 of the 60,000 beads sampled near-black fur pixels with stray channel values; fixed in the shader.
- **Tilt tipping back during the fall:** the tilt was a camera move, so the camera easing back tilted the rings too. Moved the tilt onto the head (rotation sign order fixed after a first mistake).
- **Light dark while the head was on show:** laps started and ended with the light in front and the Softness fader sped it up mid-lap; reoriented so the light is behind at lap boundaries and in front mid-hold.
- **Embed start-up crash:** `wake()` ran before the off-screen flag's `let` line (TDZ); switched to `var offScreen`.
- **Downloads blocked:** macOS refused Claude access to `~/Downloads` (Operation not permitted, even unsandboxed); the user moved the files into the project.
- **Server limits:** Claude Code's permission check wouldn't let Claude stop the user's `dvs` on port 8080 (PID 49748), so a second copy runs on port 8083 with the new save routes (my copy restarted a couple of times via TaskStop).
- **Live check redirects:** the first live check got a 308 to www; it needed `curl -L` with the www host.
- **Type scale `rem` breakage:** experiments setting base size on `html` shrank the whole scale; fixed by pinning the root to 16px.
- **Phone menu wouldn't reproduce:** headless Chrome (touch, coarse pointer, reduced motion), Playwright WebKit and the live site all kept the menu open; an iOS simulator booted but couldn't be tapped from here. The user's answers (Menu key, iPhone Safari, every time) pointed at a Safari-only rendering cause, and the bar's `overflow-x: clip` was the one thing cutting off the hanging panel. The fix is inferred, not reproduced: the first real confirmation will be the user's phone.

## Lessons learned
- For a bead-cloud head, depth cues matter more than point count: perspective, a three-quarter view and normals blended across faces. Geometry smoothing destroys small features on a low-poly model.
- Lab version files (`*-versions.js`, here `tinkering/tatankagram/versions.js`) are written by the user while Claude works; never restore or test-write them (per memory).
- The user's lab rules apply: Matte chrome, a typed number field on every numeric control, saved versions through the lab server.
- Timing rules the user wants to hold regardless of settings should be derived from the loop clock (the light laps), not set by independent fixed times.
- The user likes tunable controls for everything moving (easing, light, ring motion) rather than fixed choices.
- When testing form-key behaviour, check the key's label right before clicking; the loop may already have formed the head.
- A page-level computed-font-size audit in headless Chrome is the reliable way to prove a type scale is enforced; it also exposed the `html` base-size bug.
- Small x-height serifs (Newsreader) need scaling to match Söhne's lowercase height for a fair comparison.
- `overflow: clip` on a bar clips anything that hangs out of it, on iPhone Safari even mid-animation. Trim one axis with `clip-path: inset()` instead.
- Playwright's WebKit (installed in a scratch folder) is the quickest way here to tap a page as Safari would.
