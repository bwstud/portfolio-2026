## Challenges and how we solved them
- **The white edge didn't show in headless Chrome.** The cause is the global rule `:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--fg); outline-offset: 3px; }` (`styles.css:204`). `showModal()` focuses the dialog, and the owner's browser matched `:focus-visible` there. Headless Chrome doesn't for a mouse-opened dialog. Forcing that outline onto `.sheet` reproduced the screenshot exactly: a pale line 3px outside the rounded edge.
- **Nested scrolling under Lenis.** The sheet's details glide through Lenis, which would have taken the wheel over the side column. `smooth-scroll.js` already honours `[data-lenis-prevent]` for the sheet instance (line 32), so the side column got that attribute. Checked at 1440×700: a wheel over the side moved only the side (scrollTop 184, its full extent), and a wheel over the details moved only the details. The side stayed at top 76px throughout.

## Lessons learned
- The owner doesn't want scrollbars shown by default: both sheet columns now hide them, and the taste rule was corrected.
- Any element that takes focus programmatically (dialogs) shows the site-wide focus ring in some browsers. Give it `:focus-visible { outline: none }` when its edge isn't a control.
