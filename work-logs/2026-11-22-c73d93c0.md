## Work done

### 1. First build (shipped, PR #48)

New lab `tinkering/diffusion-studio/` (now `experiments/diffusion-studio/`), seven files:

- `shaders.js`: the rendering. Passes per frame:
  - source: the words at their depth, as transmittance and emission, each with coverage and coverage-weighted depth.
  - pyramids: each image blurred into its own mip levels at doubling radii (2, 4, 8 … px), so any radius can be read (B-spline upsampling).
  - state: the pane's own record of condensation, water film and grease, painted by the pointer and the drops.
  - composite: the material and its conditions decide how far light spreads and how much passes straight through; words read from the pyramids at the radius their depth calls for; lamps drawn analytically, already spread (exact erf for rectangles); front light and room reflection on the pane.
  - final: orbit perspective, bloom, exposure, white balance, tone curve, split tone, vignette, grain.
- `app.js`: pipeline, camera, drops, pointer tools, fonts, undo, looks.
- `schema.js`: every setting with its default, the panel, 12 materials, 15 looks.
- `ui.js`: panel builder. Every fader has a typed number field (in range takes effect as typed; leaving tidies; typed values kept off-step; accessible name "…, typed") and a Reset when moved off the look's value.
- `text.js`: sets the words on a canvas through the camera: letters, neon tube centre lines, and a per-letter id spread past each letter's edge.
- `gl.js`: WebGL2 helpers, half-float mip pyramids ping-ponged between two textures.
- `index.html`: Matte chrome, stage with a tool bar and view controls, tabs, side panel.

Technology (answered for the user): no Three.js and no libraries; plain JavaScript modules on raw WebGL2 with hand-written GLSL fragment shaders and no build step. Words are set with the browser's 2D canvas, which is why any installed font works. It doesn't simulate rays; it uses one physical rule, that light spreads in proportion to its distance behind the surface, which is cheap enough for 60 fps at retina size.

The model: a diffusing surface spreads each point of light into a disc that grows with the point's distance behind it, so whatever touches the glass is sharp. Depth is per pixel (per letter, tilt, scatter, wave, and a come-and-go animation with letter stagger), so letters swim into focus as they reach the glass. Shadows of opaque letters also soften with the key lamp's size (penumbra) and grow toward the lamp.

- **13 scenarios:** silhouette, pressed (contact sharp, edges falling away), lit object, stencil, glowing, neon, halo-lit, gel, LED matrix, projected (focused on the pane, so sharp on frost and invisible on clear), written in fog, breath reveal (grease shows only when the glass fogs), smoke.
- **12 materials:** frosted, sandblasted, opal acrylic, rice paper, tracing paper, voile, reeded, hammered, glass block, ice, cathedral, clear. Scatter, straight-through, glow tail, tint, absorption, micro-relief, texture, refraction, reflection, cleared by water.
- **7 arrangements** of two panes: one, side by side, band, graded, stacked (with gap), checkerboard, louvres; frame bars.
- **Conditions:** condensation with beads, regrow and clearing rates, breath; rain beads and running drops (CPU drops with stick-slip, trails clear the fog); wet patches (water clears frost); dust; fingerprints (oil clears frost, resists fog); scratches that glint toward the front light; water marks; frost from the edges; cracks with per-shard refraction; heat shimmer.
- **Light:** two lamps behind (bulb, softbox, window with bars, blinds, strip, ring; EV, kelvin, gel, position, distance, size, rotation, flicker, motion), falloff, light on the letters, light on your side, room wall and wash, haze, room reflection.
- **Camera:** zoom about the pointer (wheel or pinch, − / + / Fit, keys − + 0), pan (Pan tool, Space-drag, middle button), orbit and roll (Orbit tool, Option-drag to roll). Orbit looks at the flat render in perspective; light passing straight through clear glass shifts with the view (parallax), light scattered by frost stays on the surface. The surface state lives on the pane, so wipes stay put. Pane width, height and surrounding wall are settings. Bokeh for what is behind the pane only, scaled with zoom. Exposure, white balance, tone curve, contrast, saturation, bloom, halation, fringing, vignette, grain, split tone, render scale. Camera controls live in a new Camera tab.
- **Type:** any font by family, an Installed fonts button (Local Font Access) listing families and exact faces; weight, italic, width, size in cm, tracking, line height, word spacing, alignment, case incl. small caps, kerning, ligatures, outline, horizontal scale, slant; placement across, up, rotation, depth, two tilts, mirror, shadow perspective.
- **Tools:** Move the words, Pan, Orbit, Wipe, Wet, Breathe, Grease, Clean, each with a tooltip (the user had asked what they do; Wipe and Breathe only show an effect with condensation). Shift-scroll pushes the words in depth.
- **Undo:** Cmd+Z / Cmd+Shift+Z over settings, a burst of changes (a drag) as one step; text fields keep their own undo. Surface paint is not in it.
- **Looks:** 15 starting scenes; the selector shows and remembers the chosen look. Settings are remembered in this browser.
- **Experiments page:** card at the top of the Experiments index, thumbnail `thumb.jpg` (960×600) from the rainy-window look. The card edit sat alongside the user's own uncommitted changes in that file.
- Space now pans; play/pause moved to P.

- `index.html`: header buttons (Versions, Compare, Mutate, Render still), timeline strip, popovers, `.is-keyed` markers, `#view.is-still` styling.

Features, as built:

- **Word layers:** three more lines of words, each with its own depth, font (a select: "Same as the main words" or any known font), colour and mode (shadow, lit, glowing, gel). The pane fit includes them.
- **Pattern in the light (gobo):** leaves (rebuilt as two overlapping layers of larger leaves so they read), window bars, blinds, lace, water caustics, branches, chain-link.
- **Light shafts:** ray-marched at quarter resolution through the haze, Henyey-Greenstein phase. Shadow rays go through the words, layers and pattern. The eye's own view is also dimmed by the planes it passes, so haze behind a stencil wall shows only through the letters.
- **Lens:** focus distance with The glass / The words / The light buttons, camera distance, and lens blur. The glass itself blurs when focus is behind it. Four looks were retuned for the new lens scale.
- **Render still:** a path tracer through the glass with lens blur and focus distance, accumulating passes in float textures. You choose the shape (stage, 1:1, 4:5, 3:2, 16:9, 9:16, 2:1), the long edge and the pass count (typed fields). A frame guide on the stage shows the crop. Progress bar, Stop / Back to live, Save PNG.
- **Timeline** (`timeline.js`): play, to start, typed playhead and length, Loop, and a "Key changes" record toggle. Each key has its own ease (smooth, linear, hold, in, out). You can drag keys, double-click to add one, delete a key, or remove a whole track. Numbers ease, colours blend, everything else holds. Track names carry their group ("Light behind · Across"). Keyed settings get a small mark in the panel. Export video records one pass to mp4 or webm with MediaRecorder on `cvs.captureStream(60)`. Everything is stored in `S.tl`, so undo and versions include it.
- **Versions** (`saved.js`): name and save with a thumbnail, then load, compare, or delete (inline confirm, no browser dialogs). Export all and Import as JSON. Stored in localStorage under `tinkering-versions:diffusion-studio`; no `*-versions.js` file is touched.
- **Compare:** splits the stage with a draggable line. The left side renders the other settings (with their own words and layers) using the same view.
- **Mutate:** nudges 5–9 of the faders and colours you can currently see. Cmd+Z reverts.
- **Conditions:** fog creeps back over time, warmth clears fog and melts frost, steam in the air.

## Challenges and how we solved them

- **Repeatable frames:** a headless Chrome script over CDP (session scratchpad `shot.mjs`, plus a 2× variant) froze time and shot contact sheets of all looks after every round.
- **Looks failing on the first sheets** (blown-out area lights, bulb sharp through frost, brown bevel edges on silhouettes, pepper-dot rain, black glass-block centres, geometric paper fibres, frost as dark smudges, invisible smoke, a dark disc from a specular branch, shoji letters vanishing under steep tilt plus large scatter): area-light EVs lowered, frosted clarity set to 0, `in.ev` default −5; bead rim and lens strength softened and the fine mist beads limited to condensation; the specular branch replaced with smoothstep; fibres warped and softened; scattering surface detail now glows with the light behind it; smoke made emissive and wispy; glass-block refraction lowered and its look moved to wall light; scatter angles and tilt reduced so depth ranges are playable.
- **Drop tails ran the wrong way:** the elongation was on the leading side; flipped.
- **Camera across every pass:** every pass now maps render uv to pane cm through one affine (zoom, pan, roll); orbit is a homography in the final pass over the bounding box the tilted view needs; parallax is a constant slope from the eye's offset, applied only to the straight-through path.
- **Looks selector stayed on "Looks":** the change handler reset it to ''; it now keeps and remembers the choice.
- **Font picker showed nothing:** the suggestion list was filtered by the current value (section 2).
- **Pane-fit switch didn't refresh rows:** added `pane.fit` to the visibility list in `changed()`.
- **Shafts seemed missing:** they were working, but with the lamp straight behind the words the beams point at the camera and show only as glow. Haze behind an opaque stencil also showed through the wall; the march now multiplies by what the eye sees through.
- **Leaf pattern didn't read:** too small and sparse; now two layers of larger leaves.
- **Traced still was noisy, dark and less blurred than live:** scattered rays rarely hit a small bright lamp. They now take the lamp already spread analytically (smooth lamp for diffuse rays), like the live view; live and traced match closely and the still is clean at 200 passes.
- **Unselected timeline keys were invisible:** Matte's button reset cleared their background; the key selector now sits under `.matte`. Track names also gained their group names.
- **Test-script glitch:** `const` redeclaration in headless test code; wrapped in blocks.
- **Live check after the first merge got redirects:** followed them with `curl -L` to confirm the live page, scripts and card load (the site lives on the www domain).
- **Redirect missed folder URLs on the live site:** Vercel's `/tinkering/:path*` doesn't match paths ending in a slash, so `/tinkering/` and `/tinkering/peek-lab/` gave 404 after #49. PR #50 switched to `/tinkering/(.*)` → `/experiments/$1`; every old form now gives 308 to the new place, from www and the bare domain.

## Lessons learned

- Frozen-time headless screenshots and contact sheets of every look after each round catch regressions quickly.
- A suggestion list (datalist) filters by the text already in the box; for a long installed-font list a search field plus a visible list works better.
- The user's stage is nearly square, so any fit-to-view logic must use the real stage aspect.
- Vercel `:path*` redirect patterns miss trailing-slash paths; `/(.*)` form works. Always verify redirects on the live site after a merge.
- Matte's button reset overrides plain class selectors; custom controls need selectors scoped under `.matte`.
- Offer judgement calls (auto-condensation, shrink-the-words alternative, trimming the scrollbar line) and let the user decide; the user says "Yes, do that" / "Ship it" when ready. Merging to main is live, so ship only when told.
- Storage keys keyed by old paths or names are worth keeping when renaming, to avoid hiding saved data.
