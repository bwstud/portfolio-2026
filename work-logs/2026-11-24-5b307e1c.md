- Smaller frames (576 wide) would cut about another 20% but soften text. Offered, not done.
- `record.mjs` now writes both encodes, and `ENCODE_ONLY=1` re-encodes from the raw recordings without re-recording.

## Challenges and how we solved them

- **Cards blurred until mid-window:** the scroll-linked ranges ended at `cover 40–50%`. Replaced with a trigger-then-play entrance.
- **Scroll push froze the Mazlan wall:** running a CSS animation backwards from `currentTime` 0 finishes it, and the browser drops it. Fixed by moving the clock many loops forward; the whole push was then reverted anyway.
- **Names arriving far too late** (screen recording): the name plate was driven by the board's lamp (a third down), not the reveal (an eighth up). Fixed with one sequence and `recordreveal`.
- **Titles and tags out of order:** the ink's invisible opening delayed titles, and tags sat visible ahead of everything. Fixed by skipping the first 20% of the ink and holding tags until the title is legible.
- **A link to a later record left an earlier one blank:** an instant jump crosses from below to above without an IntersectionObserver callback. Fixed with `settle()`.
- **Menu misaligned:** each row reserved an invisible lamp lane before its text. Fixed with a left lamp lane and a padded head.
- **Cover texture hidden:** the WebGL plate and field shaders painted opaque Night. Made them transparent.
- **Glint too fast, then invisible in captures:** the first version crossed the letters in under 100ms. After slowing it, timed screenshots missed it, so progress was logged per frame in-page, the path tightened, and screenshots taken at paused points.
- **Tests ignored scrolling:** under Lenis, `window.scrollTo` may not take effect. Use `siteLenis.scrollTo(y, {immediate: true})`.
- **Metal gradient dull:** the light bands didn't line up with the capitals. Realigning them didn't save the look, and the user rejected it.
- **"Lost" text sequence:** not reproduced. See Unsolved problems.
- **Playwright's bundled Chromium can't play H.264:** wall playback was tested in real Chrome.
- **No `cwebp`-style encoder found for posters:** posters are made with Chrome.
- **Mazlan Home clip caught by the "What's your role?" welcome:** the setup now dismisses it before filming.
- **Apps and spec-preview clips missed their controls:** found the right selectors and re-recorded them.
- **Engineer health clip didn't return to its first frame:** given a dissolve at the loop point and re-recorded.
- **Prototypes have moved on since the stills:** the Groom Lake audit checklist page no longer exists (filmed the record's side panels instead). The History tab was unreadable at tile size (replaced with the theme switch). A clip tile shows the clip's first frame rather than the old still, and the original stills are untouched.
- **A blocked `rm -f`** on a scratchpad frame folder was not retried; a new folder was used.
- **Central log location:** this project's central logs sit directly in `~/.claude/work-logs/` and also in `~/.claude/work-logs/Portfolio 2026/`. Copies were kept in both.
- **No browser to watch in:** the Chrome extension never connected, so checks were headless Playwright logs and screenshots (scripts in the session scratchpad, local server `localhost:8080`).

## Lessons learned

- The user's names: "project name" is the small mono name (MAZLAN BUILDER), "tagline" is the large title, "attr tags" is the mono tag list, and "info cards" are the fields.
- The user doesn't want scroll speed or the pointer moving ambient pieces. Entrances should be tight and in reading order.
- When two scripts animate one block, give them one clock. Separate triggers drift apart.
- Menu rules: the header aligns with the items under it and is proportional to the rows; rows have seams and lamps; menus never scroll; rows get no magnetic surface.
- Shading under a docked element should have no hard or solid edge.
- For the board: things leaving should fade before the frame shrinks, hover shouldn't light anything the pointer isn't on, and highlights should fade rather than snap. Hover hit areas must span a row's gaps or they blink.
- A painted-on metal text gradient was rejected; a one-time glint was what the user chose to try. No verdict on the glint yet.
- Changing Matte changes every menu that uses it. Say so when shipping.
- Drift-wall clips: no cursor. A mix of moving and still screens looked odd to the user, even though Claude had argued it would look natural; every screen should move. Loops should lean toward 12s.
- Both prototypes are fully coded, in `~/Projects/Personal/`, so clips can be re-recorded by script.
- For interface video, AV1 at the same SSIM is about 40% the size of H.264, but use it only where decoding is in hardware when many play at once.
- Builder clips take about a minute each to record because setup plays the story up to the right moment. Groom Lake clips take about 40s each.
