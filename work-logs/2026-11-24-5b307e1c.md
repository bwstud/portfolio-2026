## Challenges and how we solved them

- **Cards blurred until mid-window:** the scroll-linked ranges ended at `cover 40–50%`. Replaced with a trigger-then-play entrance.
- **Scroll push froze the Mazlan wall** (tried and reverted): running a CSS animation backwards from `currentTime` 0 finishes it and the browser drops it. Fixed by keeping the clock many loops in, then the whole push was reverted at Brian's request.
- **Names arriving far too late** (Brian's screen recording): the name plate was driven by the board's lamp (a third down the window), not the reveal (an eighth up). Fixed by driving everything from one sequence and flipping the plate on `recordreveal`.
- **Link straight to a later record left an earlier one blank:** an instant jump crosses from below to above without an IntersectionObserver callback. Fixed with `settle()` on earlier records.
- **No browser to watch in:** the Chrome extension was never connected; all checks were headless Playwright timing logs and screenshots (scripts in the session scratchpad).

## Lessons learned

- Brian's names: "project name" = the small mono name (MAZLAN BUILDER); "tagline" = the large title; "attr tags" = the mono tag list; "info cards" = the fields.
- He doesn't want scroll speed or the pointer moving ambient pieces (the lean, the push). Entrances should be tight and in reading order.
- When two scripts animate one block, give it one clock. Separate triggers drift apart.
