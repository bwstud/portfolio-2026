## Work done

### Research

- *Wicker* (Sundance 2026, wide release Oct 2026; Eleanor Wilson and Alex Huston Fischer). Wētā Workshop built the wicker husband: 3D-printed appliances finished as cane over a spandex suit, a facial prosthetic that moves with the face, carved-wood dentures, muka (flax) hair and lashes, and a little VFX light through the weave "to show he's hollow".
- Read Wētā's project page and looked at its stills (downloaded to the scratchpad only, never into the repo). What the face actually does:
  - a fine 2/2 twill of flat, glossy cane strips, honey to umber, with dark gaps;
  - concentric rings round the eye sockets, a herringbone braid down the nose, cheek strands curving round the nose, a grid on the forehead;
  - visible seams where appliances meet;
  - real human eyes, flax brows, a shaggy flax beard and windswept hair;
  - wider herringbone panels on the neck and body.
- Prior art: no photo-to-basketry filter turned up. The building blocks are AR face filters on MediaPipe's face mesh, line integral convolution for fibre, and flow-aligned texture synthesis.

### The experiment: `experiments/wicker/`

- **How it works:**
  - MediaPipe tasks-vision 1.2.2 runs on the device: face landmarker (478 points, up to 4 faces), hair segmenter and selfie segmenter. The models are vendored in `models/` (4.8 MB in all). The wasm comes from jsdelivr.
  - `weave.js` is a WebGL2 renderer. Each face is drawn as MediaPipe's canonical mesh with its UVs (`mesh.js`, generated from `canonical_face_model.obj`, Apache 2.0). The weave is designed once in UV space, so it follows any face in any pose.
- **Panels:** forehead grid, cheek arcs round the nose, chin diagonal (nearest seed wins). Eye rings, the mouth ring and the nose herringbone claim a band over whatever panel is under them. A wrapped binding cane runs wherever two panels meet.
- **The cane:** twill or plain over and under, with per-strand tone and grain, strands that dip at the end of each run, and occlusion.
  - Lit by the photo's own light: a least-squares fit of landmark luminance against mesh normals, plus the photo's broad shading.
  - Antialiased, and the strand count is capped per face so no strand drops under 4.5 px.
- **Holes:** the eye openings are left open, so the real eyes show. The mouth opening becomes carved wooden teeth.
- **Around the face:**
  - An outer ring reaches into the hairline.
  - Bare skin elsewhere (neck, hands, arms) gets a wider herringbone. Skin is a chroma model from the face, with brightness and saturation checks so white shirts stay out, and highlights judged by the skin around them.
  - Hair becomes straw combed along the photo's structure tensor (LIC). Stray straws stand out from the hair edge.
  - Optional flax beard (short or full), flax brows, and "light through the weave" (patches of warm light in the gaps).
- **Grades:** Golden hour, Riverbank, Village, As taken.
- **Input:** choose a photo, drop or paste one, or use the camera.
  - The camera is a live, mirrored wicker mirror. The shutter weaves the frame at full size.
  - Small photos are scaled up to 1600 px so the strands stay crisp.
  - If no face is found, it looks again in a crop round the top of the person.
  - Straw is only allowed within reach of a face, so a black shirt doesn't turn to straw.
- **Reveal:** the weave grows out from the tip of the nose strand by strand, then skin, hair, beard and stray straws (2.4 s). With reduced motion it appears at once.
- **UI (Matte via `rig.css`):**
  - "Sit for the basket weaver" intro with a woven mark.
  - Strands fader and Stray straws fader, each with a typed field kept in step both ways (per the tuning-lab rule).
  - Pattern, Cane, Beard and Light radio groups (arrow keys work), and a Light through the weave key.
  - Save (JPEG), Share (Web Share where files are supported), Hold to compare, New photo, Camera.
  - Keys: C compares, S saves, space takes the portrait.
  - Status lamp and caption. Settings are remembered per viewer. The phone layout stacks the stage over the panel.
- **Notes card:** the film's method, and how this one does the same.
- **Experiments index:** a Wicker Portrait card added first, with `thumb.jpg`. The thumbnail is a faceless crop (nose to collar) of a stock test render, so no one is identifiable.
- **`experiments/notes.js`:** the notes card head was set in capitals (`text-transform: uppercase`). Per the standing no-caps rule it is now sentence case. This changes every experiment's notes card.

### Measured (headless Chrome for Testing on this Mac, so software-ish GL; not real devices)

- Still photos weave in about 400–600 ms once models are cached. A cold first run took 7.3 s.
- The live mirror ran at about 48 frames a second: detection, both masks and the weave every frame, fed by a y4m fake webcam made from a test photo. The shutter weaves at full size in about 310 ms.
- No page errors in the UI, phone (390 px, no sideways scroll), live and shutter runs. The typed strands field moved the fader both ways.

## Challenges and how we solved them

- **The forehead panel swallowed the eye rings.** Features now add the strongest base panel's claim to their own, so their band always wins and the seam sits on the band's edge.
- **Rings melted into flat blobs.** The mouth's ring radius was squashed vertically. Rings are now spaced by distance from the ellipse, and a gradient bug (an extra divide) that had let the eyes claim the whole face was fixed.
- **White shirts woven as skin:** added brightness against the face, a saturation check, and skin judged on a blurred photo.
- **Holes in the weave at highlights:** the outer ring now needs only "person and not hair". Body skin judges bright pixels by the coarser skin around them.
- **The beard looked like a broom, then knitted:** the smooth noise was sampled at too high a frequency (locks a few pixels wide). Rescaled, with slow lock-to-lock wander and depth shading.
- **Live faces too small for 60 strands:** a per-face strand cap, and live mode runs at 1280 px on desktop.
- **The ad-hoc python server and a combined setup command were blocked** by the permission classifier. Testing used the project's own `node tools/lab-server.mjs`.
- **Profile faces:** MediaPipe's finder needs both eyes. The message says so, and nothing is woven rather than guessing.

## Unsolved problems

- Not tried on real phones or a real webcam: iPhone Safari camera, HEIC, mobile GPU speed. The live numbers come from headless Chrome only.
- Specular glints on skin can still leave small unwoven spots on hands.
- Glasses get no treatment; the weave runs under the frames.
- The first visit downloads about 9 MB (wasm about 4 MB compressed, plus models). The page starts loading on open and shows "Setting up the loom".
